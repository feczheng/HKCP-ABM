Behaviour and outputs

The public edition uses the original school-allocation, directed routing, movement and live-accounting engines with a synthetic walking-only home-school-home schedule. Survey-calibrated mode choice, restricted-POI visits, multimodal paths, policy labs, stored 2D/3D experiments, reports and manuscripts are not included. Public output values are not interchangeable with the scientific version.

Real geography does not make homes, pupil enrolment, schedules, floor assignments or activity estimates observed. Endpoint access connectors are explicitly modelled. Agents without an assigned connected school walk stay at home; no replacement mechanised trip is invented. Raw network elevation anomalies, access restrictions and disconnected components remain relevant limitations. METy and MVPA counters are model estimates using public reference values and priors, not clinical or externally validated measurements.
